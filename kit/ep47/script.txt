[confident] Up twenty percent. Sit out next year? Cash out, A? Or stay in, B? Watch the very last year. Everyone says it: what goes up must come down. So after every twenty percent year, he sits in cash all of the next one. Twenty ten: he skips it. The market climbs anyway. Twenty fourteen, twenty eighteen, twenty twenty: same move. Four of the six years he skipped still went up. Then twenty twenty-two. He's in cash for a bear market. Finally, the skeptic looks smart. [curious] So big years really do warn us? And that very last year? After a huge twenty twenty-three, he skips twenty twenty-four. It soars twenty-five percent. Without him. [dramatic pause] The scoreboard. Sitting out: forty-six thousand six hundred. Staying in: seventy thousand two hundred. [firm] Big years aren't warnings. They're just big years.
