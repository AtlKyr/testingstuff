[intense] Ten percent off a record: six times. Bail out, A? Or ride it out, B? One Christmas Eve decides it. Friday, the S and P slid into another correction. The rule: when SPY closes ten percent under its record, sell. Buy back at minus twenty, or at a new record. Two thousand eight: he sells early and buys back cheaper. Twenty fifteen: no crash comes. He buys back higher. Early twenty eighteen: same story, higher again. That fall, another slide, deep into December. [curious] Remember that Christmas Eve? SPY closed twenty point two percent under its record. Two tenths past his line. He bought back cheap. Twenty twenty, twenty twenty-two: two more bargains. [dramatic pause] Final score: buy and hold, seventy thousand. The correction rule, eighty-five thousand. It won. Move his line two tenths, and it loses.
