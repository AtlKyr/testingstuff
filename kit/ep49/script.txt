[intense] Inflation, three point eight percent. Sell stocks? Cash out, A? Or hold, B? Keep an eye on one December. The rule: when inflation runs above three percent, sell. When it cools, buy back. Fast forward to twenty twenty-one. Prices heat up. It sells in June. Twenty twenty-two: stocks tumble, and it's sitting in cash. Genius? Then the rally comes without it. It buys back in twenty twenty-four, paying more than it sold for. [curious] Now, about that December. December two thousand seven. Inflation tops three percent, two months after the market's peak. It sells. It sits out the worst of the crash, and buys back over forty percent cheaper. [dramatic pause] Final count. Inflation seller: eighty-nine thousand five hundred. Just holding: seventy thousand two hundred. [firm] It won. But take away that one December, and it loses.
