[curious] Red January. Skip the next eleven months? Skip, A? Or stay, B? Wall Street's old saying: as January goes, so goes the year. Keep an eye on one quiet January. The rule: red January, sell in February, sit in cash until next January. Ten thousand dollars, twenty years. January two thousand eight: red. The rule sells and dodges the crash. By that November, it's way ahead. [dry] Two thousand nine: red again. The rule sits out, and the market rockets without it. In ten red Januaries, the rest of the year rose eight times. [intense] Now, that quiet January. Twenty twenty: January ends down four hundredths of a percent. The rule sells. The rest of the year climbs over fifteen percent. [firm] Twenty years later. The barometer: twenty-nine thousand nine hundred. Just holding: seventy thousand two hundred. January only predicted January.
