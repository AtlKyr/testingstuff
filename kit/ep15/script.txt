[curious] You have ten thousand dollars to invest. All in today, or drip it in monthly for a year? A: all in. B: the drip. One starting month changes everything. [confident] The rule: every starting month since two thousand five is one round. Two thousand five and six. All-in takes round after round. Its money simply works longer. [intense] Then the crash. The dripper wins nineteen rounds in a row, buying cheaper every month. After that, all-in runs away again. [curious] Remember that one starting month? June two thousand eight. A year later, all-in had sixty-nine hundred dollars left. The dripper, ninety-six hundred. [firm] Final score. All-in won one hundred seventy-seven rounds. The dripper, fifty-one. Average lead after a year: five hundred thirty-three dollars. [dry] Dripping isn't a better return. It's insurance, and you pay for it.
