[intense] Up four point seven percent. Buy now? Jump in, A? Or wait, B? Watch one March morning. The S and P's best day since April twenty twenty-five. SPY did this twenty-seven times since two thousand five. Each time, twins get ten thousand dollars. A buys the next morning. B waits for a drop back below the start. A year later, we compare. Two thousand eight: the dip comes back every time. B pulls way ahead. B wins nineteen of twenty-seven rounds. So patience pays? [dramatic] That March morning? Two thousand nine. A day after the bottom. The dip never came. B sat in cash all year. Twenty twenty, same story. [dramatic pause] All rounds added up: jumping in, three hundred fifty-one thousand. Waiting, three hundred forty-one thousand. Twenty times, the dip came back. The other seven? The bottom was already in.
