[serious] A million dollars, retired at the top. Broke by now, A? Or richer, B? And watch his twin. October ninth, two thousand seven: the S and P's record close. He follows the famous four percent rule: forty thousand the first year, then raised with inflation. All in SPY. His twin too. Then two thousand eight hits. By March two thousand nine, the million is down to four hundred eleven thousand. And the paychecks keep coming. Here the twins split. [dramatic] And that twin? He sold everything in November two thousand eight, after losing half. Safe in cash. In twenty nineteen, his money ran out. Our retiree never sold. Back above a million? Only in twenty fourteen. [dramatic pause] End of twenty twenty-four: two point three million left, after taking out eight hundred twenty-two thousand. Worst possible start. Still richer.
