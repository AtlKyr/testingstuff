[intense] Black Friday stocks: up just ten of twenty. Doubled, A? Or four times, B? Every Black Friday since two thousand five: a thousand dollars into SPY, instead of the mall. One Black Friday will steal the show. Then two thousand eight hits. Six years in, the pile is barely back to even. It doubles for the first time in twenty seventeen. Twenty twenty: a third gone in a month. [curious] Now, about the scary one. Black Friday, two thousand eight. Stocks are deep in a crash. Nobody's buying. That thousand dollars? It grows almost nine times, the best buy of all twenty. [dramatic pause] Final tally. Twenty thousand in. Seventy-nine thousand nine hundred out. [firm] Four times, a hundred thirty-eight dollars short. The best Black Friday deal was never in a store.
