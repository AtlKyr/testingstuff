[intense] Triple leverage. Down over eighty-five percent in one year. Triple fund, A? Or plain S and P, B? One strange year explains it. Ten thousand dollars each, starting two thousand five. The triple fund moves three times the S and P, every single day. Two thousand seven: triple's ahead. Then the crash. By March two thousand nine, its ten thousand is eight hundred dollars. Game over? It crawls back. Eight years just to catch the plain S and P. Then twenty twenty: down seventy-six percent in a month. [curious] Now, that strange year. Twenty eleven. The S and P went basically nowhere. The triple fund? Lost eleven percent. Every up-and-down day quietly eats it. [dramatic pause] Twenty years later: plain S and P, seventy-one thousand. Triple, one hundred seventy-four thousand. [firm] Triple won. If you could stomach losing ninety-two percent.
