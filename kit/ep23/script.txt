Every trader hears it: always use a stop-loss. What if you put a ten percent stop on the whole market? A, it protects you. B, it costs you. One number hides in the sells. The rule: sell after a ten percent drop from the top, buy back after a ten percent bounce. Two thousand eight. The stop fires early, and the stop-loss investor pulls ahead. Then the whipsaws start. Twenty eighteen: sold, then bought back higher. Twenty twenty: out before the worst, back near the bottom. [excited] Ahead again! Remember that hidden number? Fifteen sells. Nine times, it bought back higher. And twenty twenty-two shook it out three times. [dramatic pause] Final score. The stop-loss: sixty-five thousand, one hundred. Buy and hold: seventy thousand, two hundred. It softened the crashes, and charged you for every false alarm.
