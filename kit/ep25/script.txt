Meet the world's worst market timer. Every year, he buys at the highest price of the year. Does he still make money? A, yes. B, no. One habit hides in his story. He invests one thousand dollars a year for twenty years. His twin buys every year at the lowest price. Two thousand seven: the worst timer buys the exact top. January two thousand eight: the top again. By March two thousand nine, he's down by half, and his twin just bought the bottom. Then twenty twenty-two. He buys on the first trading day, right before the drop. Remember his one habit? He never sold. Not in two thousand eight. Not in twenty twenty. [dramatic pause] Final score. Perfect timer: ninety-six thousand, four hundred. Worst timer: seventy-three thousand. Worst timing ever, and he still more than tripled his money.
