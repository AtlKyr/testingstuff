[intense] Dow, fifty thousand. SPY hit ten of these. Sell the milestone, A? Hold, B? One came months before two thousand eight. Each round number brings one headline: the top is in. So he sells the next morning, and buys back three months later. SPY hits two hundred. He sells. Three months later, he pays more to get back in. Two fifty, three fifty, four hundred: same story. Seven of nine times, he bought back higher. [curious] And that milestone before two thousand eight? May two thousand seven. He sells, then buys back cheaper. Genius? He was back by August, two months before the top. So the round number never saved him. [dramatic pause] Now, the totals. Milestone seller: fifty-two thousand seven hundred. Just holding: seventy thousand two hundred. [firm] Round numbers make headlines. Selling them cost over seventeen thousand.
