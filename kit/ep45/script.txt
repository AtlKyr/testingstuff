[curious] Sixty-five wins. Only twenty losses. Bollinger, A? Or just hold, B? Watch one September trade. Two bands hug the price. The rule: a close below the lower band? Buy the next morning. Back to the middle line? Sell. Ten thousand dollars in SPY, from two thousand five. Small bounces, small wins. Then two thousand eight. The market crashes. The bands keep it half in cash. By March two thousand nine, it's way ahead of buy and hold. So the bands work? [dramatic] That September trade? It bought on September fifth, two thousand eight. Then Lehman collapsed. Sold end of October: down over twenty-two percent. One loss erased more than eight average wins. After that? Cash four days out of five. [dramatic pause] Final count: the bands, about twenty thousand. Buy and hold, seventy-two thousand. Winning often isn't winning big.
