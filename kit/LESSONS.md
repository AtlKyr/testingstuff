## 1. Story structure of a ~55–60 s Short (proven in #3 and #4)
| time      | scene (bunting title)  | content |
|-----------|------------------------|---------|
| 0.0–4 s   | hook (no title yet)    | OPEN LOOP (see 1b): a surprising TRUE fact + a question/mystery, WITHOUT revealing the verdict or final score. Giant number or 2–4 word question slams in at frame 0 with punch-zoom + flash + burst + thud SFX. Rocco (detective, shocked close-up) runs in and hops on the key word. |
| 4–7 s     | title                  | (UPDATED by 1c) NO spoken branding line. Bunting/case-file tag shows "STRATEGY AUTOPSY · CASE #NN · <strategy>" silently while the VO deepens the mystery. Rocco walks in from the right (flip=True) wearing the episode costume. |
| 7–14 s    | rules                  | 2–3 rule cards that pop in ON the spoken word ("Buy…", "Sell…"), each with a check/burst and a pop SFX. Rocco points/hops per rule. |
| 14–20 s   | test                   | "Twenty years of SPY, ten thousand dollars, fees included." Price chart draws left→right, year axis, $10K coin. Rocco rises from below with a bounce (back easing). |
| 20–32 s   | results / trades       | Flip counters (win rate, avg trade, trades count) roll up on their word anchors; equity line draws; tags stamp onto the chart. Rocco paces left↔right across the screen (sine path, auto-flip). |
| 32–40 s   | twist / problem        | The surprising problem (time in cash, fees, a crash). Mood change: darker bg, rain/night, Rocco sad/sleep expression, slower music layer. |
| 40–48 s   | score                  | Final $ vs buy & hold as two big counters racing; loser turns red, winner gets god-rays. Rocco hops on each number. |
| 48–53 s   | extra knife (fees/real-world) | One more honest stress test (higher fees, slippage, worst year). |
| 53–57 s   | verdict                | Big stamp ("EDGE? NO." / "WIN RATE ≠ PROFIT") slams with stamp SFX + flash + red/green burst. Rocco smug or sad, big hop. |
| 57–60 s   | outro (REMOVED by 1c — FOLLOW text over the verdict + loop to frame 0) | "Follow for the next autopsy." Rocco bigger (px 24) jumps up from below and bounces; subscribe-style flag colors burst; captions hidden here. |

Rules of thumb
- A new scene/background every 3–7 s; something must move in EVERY frame (rain, confetti, rays, ticker band, skyline lights,
  heart_line, speed lines, drifting grid). No frame should look like a slide.
- Every number on screen appears exactly when it is spoken (word anchors), never before.
- Script ~140–155 words; after tighten + atempo the voice should be 52–58 s. #3 came out at 62.3 s — too long; trim words
  instead of speeding past 1.09.
- Spoken numbers written as words in the script ("seventy-three percent", "sixteen thousand five hundred"), otherwise
  TTS and the word anchors get messy.
- One clear "twist" per video. #4's was "73% winners, still lost to buy & hold because it sat in cash 90% of the time".
  #3's was "the dip buyer lost $29,170 versus someone who just bought every month".

The user wants more mystery / suspense so people stop scrolling and stay to the end.

- 62% of all channel views came from ADS (Demand Gen) → per-video ratios mix paid + organic; paid viewers swipe more.
- Best CTR (6%) and best avg-watched (80%) = #1, a pure curiosity title with NO result in it. Titles that reveal the
  outcome ($29,000 / "still loses") did not do better. → keep titles/covers spoiler-free.
- Long videos: open with the payoff teaser in the first 10 s (who won is hidden, but the stakes are shown), no slow intro.

WHY #2 FAILED: frame 0 was a busy 2008 price chart with axes (unreadable in half a second), the strategy name came only at
5 s, and the hook SPOILED the ending ("…and still lost to doing nothing") — no reason left to keep watching.

IN THE SHORTS FEED THE COVER IS NOT SHOWN. Frame 0 + the first ~1.5 s decide view vs swipe. So:
1. FRAME 0 must read in half a second: max 4 big words OR one giant number OR one question; Rocco big, close-up, strong
   expression (shock/detective squint); high contrast; motion and an SFX already at t=0. NO charts with axes in the first 1.5 s.
2. OPEN LOOP, NO SPOILER: the hook raises a question and hides the answer until ≥75% of the video.
   Bad: "Seventy-three percent winners. Still lost."  "It dodged 2008 and still lost to doing nothing."
   Good: "This rule dodged every crash for twenty years. Then it did something nobody expected."
         "One of these made seventy-two thousand dollars. The other made thirty-one. Guess which one is the famous strategy."
         "Seventeen out of twenty summers went up. This strategy skipped all of them. Genius… or disaster?"
3. GUESS MECHANIC: in the hook, show two big choices on screen (e.g. [A] [B], WIN? / LOSE?, SURVIVED? / DIED?) and ask the
   viewer to guess; reveal with a stamp in the verdict scene. Pinned comment: "What was your guess?"
4. MYSTERY PROPS on screen for the whole video: a CASE FILE / "PATIENT #NN" tag and a REDACTED final number "$??,???"
   (black bar or blur) pinned at the top, plus a thin "AUTOPSY PROGRESS" bar filling to 100% at the verdict. The redaction
   is torn off with a stamp + flash + impact in the final-score scene. Never show the final $ before that moment.
5. RE-HOOKS every 8–12 s, 3–6 words each, inside the word budget: "But that's not the weird part." / "Remember this number."
   / "Here's where it goes wrong." / "Now watch 2020." Pair each with a visual pattern-interrupt (wipe, punch, music switch).
6. REVEAL LATE: final score at ≥75% of the runtime, verdict stamp right after, outro ≤3 s. Optional loop ending: the last
   sentence leads back into the first one so replays feel seamless.
7. COVER (channel page/search only): consistent "CASE #NN" series look — case-file folder, strategy name, Rocco detective,
   the result REDACTED ("$??,???"), one question word (SURVIVED? / WORTH IT? / GENIUS?). Never the verdict.
8. TITLE: question or open loop, no outcome numbers ("I Tested The Turtle Breakout For 20 Years. Something Weird Happened.").
9. LOG IT: when the user reports "viewed vs swiped away" % for an episode, record it with the hook text in the
   "Hook performansı" table of `claude/short-backlog.md`; read that table before writing a new hook and copy what works.

1. THE BIG LEAK IS SECONDS 5–12 in every video (−30…−45% of viewers): that is exactly where the VO switches from the hook to
   "This is Strategy Autopsy. On the table: … The rules: …". The branding line + rules explanation kills momentum.
   → DROP the spoken "This is Strategy Autopsy. On the table: X." line. Branding stays ON SCREEN only (bunting/case-file tag).

2. #3 HELD A FLAT MIDDLE (55% from 30% to 75%): it was a RACE between two characters (dip buyer vs monthly buyer) with a
   running money counter. → Tell every episode as a race: STRATEGY vs BUY & HOLD as two runners / two piles of money moving
   through the years (2008, 2020 as obstacles), with the final totals REDACTED until the reveal. Viewers stay to see who wins.
3. #4 BLED STEADILY 8–38% (stat dump: 150 trade blips, win rate, avg trade, hold days). → ONE number per beat, max 5 numbers
   in the whole video before the reveal. Cut secondary stats.
4. THE OUTRO LOSES 10–15% (drop from ~94% onward). → No separate spoken outro scene. The verdict line is the last spoken line;

6. TOPIC PICK: simple, famous, relatable ideas win (#1 "every trading course teaches this", #5 "sell in May"). Niche
   indicators need a relatable angle in the hook or should be moved down the list.

## 2. Rocco — how to make him alive (this is what the user cares about)
Engine function: `rocco(ctx, t, costume, expr_override=None, prop=None)` + your `motion(t)` in the timing module.
`motion(t)` returns `(x, y, px, flip, moving, lift)`; the engine adds: talk mouth synced to words, blink every ~3.1 s,
breathing, walking bob (`moving=True` → bob + tilt), squash & stretch near the ground, a ground shadow that shrinks with height.

Choreography patterns that worked (copy from r2_timing.py):
- ENTRANCE every scene: walk in from off-screen `x = lerp(-180 or -450, 40, ease(prog(u,0,0.4–0.8)))` with `moving = k < 1`,
  or from the right `lerp(1150, 620, …)` with `flip=True`, or rise from below `y = lerp(1950, 1310, back(prog(u,0,0.5)))`.
  Alternate the entrance direction scene to scene.
- HOPS on key words: `lift += hop(t, T["word"], h=110–170, d=0.4–0.6)`. Put a hop on every big number and on the verdict.
  2–3 hops per scene is the sweet spot.
- PACING: during data scenes `x = 40 + (sin(u*1.4)*0.5+0.5)*520`, `moving=True`, `flip = cos(u*1.4) < 0` → he walks back and
  forth looking at the chart.
- IDLE BOUNCE: `lift = abs(sin(u*2.4))*16` when standing (never fully frozen).
- OUTRO: bigger (px 24), jumps up from below, then continuous bounce `abs(sin(u*3))*40`.
- EXPRESSIONS via `expr_override`: shock on the hook number, smug when the result is good/he "knew it", sad on losses,
  sleep in the "sitting in cash" scene, and always let "talk" through: `lambda e: "sad" if e != "talk" else e`.
- COSTUME CHANGES every scene, matched to meaning: party (hook/celebration), doctor (autopsy/title & verdict), lab (rules/test),
  crown (winner/score), night (cash/sleeping money), race (speed/trades, with prop_flag), detective + magnifier (investigation),
  fire helmet (crash), grad cap (lesson), tophat (outro/classy), none (plain sad moment).
- PROPS: prop_flag, prop_magnifier, prop_umbrella (rain/crash), prop_coin, prop_pointer(target) to point at a number.
- Default position: x≈40 (left) or 620 (right), y≈1310, px 19 → he stands BELOW the captions (captions at y≈1240) and never
  covers cards. Keep cards above y≈1180 or to the side Rocco is not on.
- Never draw or imitate any Claude/Anthropic mascot or any known character. Rocco is the only character.

## 3. Visual system
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, cairo → ffmpeg pipe (libx264 veryfast crf 17, yuv420p, bt709, +faststart).
- Fonts: Space Grotesk (headings), JetBrains Mono (numbers/tags), Inter fallback. Install them first (README); if
  `fc-list | grep -i grotesk` is empty, text silently falls back and looks wrong.
- Brand colors: green #22E58B (win), red #FF4D6A (loss), dark #05090A, plus the C dict in the engine (yellow, blue, ink, white, cream).
- Bunting (hanging pennants) with the scene title at the top of every scene except the hook.
- Captions: word-chip captions at y≈1240, hidden during the outro. Check that no card/tag sits on top of them.
- Per scene: slow push-in zoom (engine does it), color wipe transition between scenes, `PUNCH` times (zoom kick) on 5–7 big
  moments, `FLASH` on 1–2 biggest moments, `EVENTS` bursts (confetti/sparks) at 15–20 word anchors.
- Backgrounds rotate: gradient, grid_bg, rain_bg (losses), skyline at night (cash), god_rays (winner), ticker_band, stadium.

Mistakes that already happened — check for them in the contact sheet:
- Overlaps: "EVEN WORSE" card, "DID NOTHING" label, "ALL IN", "20 YEARS", "AVG HOLD" tags and a "+$533" card all overlapped other
  elements or captions and had to be moved. Before the final render, make a contact sheet (~15 frames, include frames right
  after every tag/card appears) and LOOK at it.
- Unreadable text: white text on the cream background (fix: dark ink text or a dark pill behind it).
- Overflowing text: long labels like "GOLDEN CROSS" ran off the card. Measure with `tw(ctx, s, size)` and shrink the size until it fits.
- Wrong word anchors: `wt("word", nth)` picks the nth occurrence — words like "zero, sold, fast, cash, market, RSI, just, three, ten"
  appear several times; print the words list with times and verify every anchor you use.
- Wrong numbers on screen: in the long video a crash counter showed -9% instead of the real drawdown because it used the wrong
  start date. Every displayed number must come from the backtest output files, not typed by hand.
- Empty-looking scene: a scene with only one small card looked dead; always add a second moving element (chart, counter, prop, weather).

## 4. Backtest lessons
- SPY 2005-01-03 → 2024-12-31. Adjust Open/High/Low by Adj Close/Close. Signal at close, fill next day's open, 0.05% per side, cash 0%.
- Buy & hold reference (first open → last adj close) ≈ $70–71K from $10K, max DD ≈ -55%.
- WARM-UP ARTIFACT: RSI(14) computed with EWM shows 0 on day 2 and created a fake first buy in #1. Always set signals to NaN for
  the first 60 bars (or the indicator length × 3) — `sig.iloc[:60] = np.nan`.
- Count time in market, trades (round trips), win rate, avg trade %, avg hold days, max DD, CAGR, final $. Also run a fee stress
  test (0.10% and 0.20% per side) — it gave #4 its strongest final punch.
- Useful verified side facts (can be reused as twists): the market was within 10% of its high 74% of the time (2005–2024);
  SPY overnight return 4.73× vs intraday 1.48×; correlation 0.73 between time in market and CAGR across 23 strategies.
- If a result is boring, the twist is usually: time in cash, fees, how it behaved in 2008/2020, or "win rate ≠ profit".

## 6b. Music rotation (user request 2026-11-04: "a different background music")
- #1–#6 all used the SAME 100 BPM Am-F-C-G chiptune bed. The user noticed and wants variety.
- Use `music.py` (kit root): `from music import make_music; M = make_music(TOTAL, STYLE, SR)` and replace the whole
  "chiptune music bed" block in your audio_XX.py with `music_drums, music_bass, music_arp = M["drums"], M["bass"], M["arp"]`.
  Stems are level-matched, so the existing section gains / ducking / loudnorm keep working.
- Styles: synthwave, lofi, funk, trap, marimba (and chip — retired for now, don't use).
- RULE: never use the same style as the previous episode; prefer one not used in the last 3. Match the mood:
  breakouts/momentum → synthwave · calm/detective/"boring but works" → lofi · sneaky tricks/heists/calendar hacks → funk ·
  crashes/stop-losses/fear → trap · seasonal/holiday/light topics → marimba.
- The music history is kept in the Project doc `claude/short-backlog.md` ("Müzik geçmişi"). Read it, pick, and append
  "#NN: <style>" when you finish. Also write the style into the episode doc.
- MUSIC CHANGES INSIDE THE VIDEO (user request 2026-11-04: "müzik kilit noktalarda değişsin"): use
  `make_music_sections(TOTAL, [(0.0, MAIN), (SC["twist"], DARK), (SC["score"], MAIN), (SC["verdict"], OTHER)], SR)`.
  2–3 switches per Short, ONLY at key story beats: (1) the twist/problem scene → a darker style (trap or lofi),
  (2) the final score / comeback → back to the main style, (3) optionally the verdict → a third style for the stamp.
  Never switch more often than every ~8 s. Each new style starts on its downbeat exactly at the scene cut; a riser
  leads in and a sub impact hits on the cut (M["fx"] — add it to the music sum). Line the visual wipe/punch up with it.
  The episode's MAIN style still follows the rotation rule above (different from the previous episode).
  Write the full plan into the episode doc and backlog, e.g. "#7: synthwave → trap (twist) → synthwave (score)".
- Optional variety inside one style: change `seed`, or transpose by editing the chord tables — but the style rule above is the minimum.
