[intense] The S and P just fell four days straight. Buy now, A? Wait for green, B? One October week decides this. Two investors add a thousand dollars after every four-day losing streak since two thousand five. A buys the next morning. B waits for the first green day. For years, they run neck and neck. [curious] Christmas, twenty eighteen. A catches the knife. B waits, and buys four percent cheaper! So patience pays? Now, that October week. Two thousand eight. A buys, and three more red days follow. B waits. Green finally comes: the biggest up day in twenty years. B buys the next morning, seven percent higher. Final score. B, waiting for green: four hundred forty-eight thousand. A, buying the drop: four hundred fifty thousand. [firm] Waiting felt safer. It just cost more.
