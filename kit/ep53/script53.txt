[curious] Down twenty percent. Buy the bear? Buy now, A? Or wait, B? Since two thousand five: four bear markets. One of them settles this. Twin A buys in with ten thousand once SPY closes twenty percent below its record. Twin B waits for a new bull: twenty percent off the lowest close. Christmas, twenty eighteen: A buys near the bottom. B pays more. Twenty twenty: A wins again. Twenty twenty-two: B waits almost a year, and pays more. [dry] Three rounds to A. [intense] Now, the bear that settles it. July, two thousand eight: A buys. Then SPY sinks below half its record. B waits for December: over a quarter cheaper. [firm] Final count, twenty twenty-four. Twin A: one hundred thirty-one thousand five hundred. Twin B: one hundred forty-seven thousand six hundred. Three wins lost to one patient wait.
