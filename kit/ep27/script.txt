[confident] You've heard it a hundred times: only buy on red days. Two investors put in five hundred dollars a month. A buys the day it arrives. B waits for a red day, down one percent. Who ends up richer? One quiet year hides the answer. The rule: same paychecks, only the buying day differs. Two thousand eight: red days everywhere. B buys the panic cheap, and he pulls ahead! But barely. Then the market calms down, and B just waits. And waits. [curious] Remember that quiet year? Two thousand seventeen had only four days down one percent. His September money sat in cash for five months, then he bought fifteen percent higher. Final score. A, buying right away: four hundred ninety thousand dollars. B, waiting for red: four hundred eighty-eight thousand. [firm] Red days don't build wealth. Paydays do.
