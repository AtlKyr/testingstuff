In two thousand eight, the seller sneaks ahead. Then come the bounces. Streak after streak, it steps aside while the market climbs. By twenty ten, its lead is gone for good. Twenty twenty? It sells into the crash, rebuys lower, and still can't catch up. Remember day six? After five red days, day six closed green twenty-seven of forty-three times. It usually sold right before a bounce. [dramatic pause] Now, the scoreboard. The seller: forty thousand nine hundred dollars. Sitting tight: seventy thousand two hundred. Five red days felt like a warning. Mostly, they were a discount.
