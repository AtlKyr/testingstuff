Fear hits thirty. Do you buy? Buy the panic, A? Or just hold, B? One quiet stretch decides it. The rule: when the fear gauge closes at thirty or higher, buy at the next open, then stay in until a calm year passes. At first, no fear, so it sits in cash. August two thousand seven, fear finally spikes, and it jumps in, right before the big crash. Ouch. It holds on and buys every scare after. Five trades. Five winners. So it wins, right? Remember that quiet stretch? From late twenty twelve to twenty fifteen, fear never showed up. Almost three years in cash, while the market climbed forty percent. [dramatic pause] Now, the scoreboard. Fear buyer: thirty-two thousand four hundred. Just holding: seventy thousand two hundred. Being greedy in fear worked. Waiting for the fear didn't.
