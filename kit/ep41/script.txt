[curious] Sixty-forty. Still the safe bet? All stocks, A? Or sixty-forty, B? One ugly year tests it. Two investors, ten thousand dollars each, two thousand five. He goes all in on SPY. She holds sixty percent SPY, forty percent ten-year Treasuries, and rebalances every January. Then two thousand eight. Stocks lose thirty-seven percent. Her mix loses just fourteen. She takes the lead and holds it for years. So bonds win? Then the bull market runs, and he passes her in twenty fourteen. Twenty twenty's crash puts her back in front, but only briefly. [dramatic] The ugly year arrives: twenty twenty-two. Stocks fall eighteen percent. Her bonds? Down sixteen. The shield cracked right when she needed it. [dramatic pause] Final count: sixty-forty, forty-four thousand. All stocks, seventy thousand. Her worst drop was half of his. His ending was far bigger.
